## Proposed route forward

- Preserve Step1 frozen checkpoint/preprocessing/action/time contracts and Step2
  fresh-replay mechanics; maintain quiescent initialization and truthful history.
- Explicitly approve the prospective observation domain, independently of
  historical provenance. Treat any change as a new protocol, not a favorable
  display/codec selected from validation outcomes.
- Obtain approval for the separately versioned instrument repair in
  `readout_refit_proposal_v1.md`; no refit is executed here. Keep original results.
- Validate natural absolute/differential physical measurements on new families,
  including non-target resolution at intended block-effect/angle scales. The
  5-world-px/.10-rad caps are fixed physical budgets; an empirical margin larger
  than a budget is a limitation, not permission to enlarge it.
- Characterize production nuisance and prespecified appearance stress controls;
  choose no lossy codec based on readout performance. Check latent physical-dose
  separation, norms/reference distributions and physical readouts jointly.
- Measure natural predictor ceiling under correct action/history contracts.
  Require meaningful measurement, not perfect forecasts. Separate encoder/
  instrument/OOD limits and source-domain attribution uncertainty from intrinsic
  forecast errors. Further matched domain-only diagnostics may be needed.
- Freeze all choices before genuinely fresh decisive validation. The current
  32 diagnostic and prior21 source families are now exposed development data
  for any revision, never its final held-out validation.

Passing these observation/measurement checks allows **review of Step3**, not
automatic H2. Ridge/P, actual patch norm, independent selectivity, random/zero/
full controls and real-C fidelity remain later mandatory gates. Do not make a
future projector a circular prerequisite for collecting its natural training
data. No PhaseII/H3 or closed-loop work is approved.
